The Digital Sangoma

sangoma (Wikipedia) n. [Zulu] Practitioner of traditional medicine in Southern Africa fulfilling different social and political roles in the community like divination, healing physical, emotional, and spiritual illnesses, directing birth or death rituals, finding lost cattle, protecting warriors, counteracting witchcraft and narrating the history, cosmology, and concepts of their tradition. Often [wrongly] referred to outside Southern Africa as 'witch doctor', they are reknowned for their traditional herbal remedies and divination by 'reading' 'bones' cast onto the ground.
